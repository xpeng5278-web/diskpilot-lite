DiskPilot Lite 0.3.0 · C: space report

1. Right-click the ZIP > Extract All. Open the extracted folder.
2. Double-click Scan-C-Drive.cmd.
3. If WizTree is missing, read the licence notice. Type Y or y and press Enter to install. Enter alone or any other answer means no installation.
4. Administrator access enables a fast scan (about 1–2 minutes). Declining switches to slow mode, which may take more than 10 minutes. Keep the window open; periodic messages confirm it is still working.
5. The report opens in your browser. Review the suggestions and decide what to clean yourself.

Suggestions only: this tool never deletes or moves your files.
It removes only the temporary CSV it generated and its newly created temporary folder if empty.
The temporary CSV needs approximately 50–200 MB, preferably on D: or otherwise in the system temporary directory. Its full path is printed. An interrupted scan may leave it behind for you to review.
Except for installing WizTree with your consent through winget, processing stays local: no network or uploads.
WizTree is free for personal use. It is not bundled because its EULA forbids redistribution without a Distribution License.

Open View-Sample-or-Import-CSV.html to view fictional sample data or import your own WizTree CSV.
Export: select C: in WizTree > Scan > Ctrl+Alt+E > save to D: or USB > drag the CSV into the report.
Use the text language button at the top right to switch languages. The browser remembers your choice when local storage is available.
Checkboxes are for planning only and never perform cleanup. Folder sizes are upper bounds, not entirely removable data. Back up important files first.
Use Windows Settings > System > Storage > Temporary files, Storage Sense, or Disk Cleanup as appropriate. Never manually delete Windows, Program Files, or WinSxS.
Save this report downloads DiskPilot-Report-YYYY-MM-DD.html to your browser Downloads folder. Press Ctrl+J to see downloads.
Scanned reports are named C-Drive-Report-<time>.html. Saved reports work offline and retain selections.
Reports contain your folder paths. Share thoughtfully. Your actual paths and filenames are preserved in either language.
The English interface has one intentional Chinese label: the button for switching to Chinese.
Windows is required for scanning. Modern browsers can view reports offline. Node.js is not required to use the product.
